What We Offer

From a simple tidy-up to a full transformation — Leo has you covered across Falmouth and within 10km.

Lawn Care

A well-kept lawn makes all the difference. Leo offers regular mowing, edging, scarifying, and seasonal treatments to keep your grass healthy, green, and beautifully presented throughout the year.

Regular & one-off available

Hedge Trimming

Overgrown hedges can make even a lovely garden look tired. Leo shapes and maintains all hedge types — box, privet, laurel, and more — with precision and a tidy finish every time.

All hedge types welcome

Pressure Washing

Patios, driveways, paths, and decking collect years of grime. Leo's pressure washing service restores hard surfaces to their best, quickly and without damage to your property.

Patios · Driveways · Decking

Potting & Planting

Whether you want colourful seasonal containers for your front door, or a new border with year-round interest, Leo will help you choose the right plants for your space and Cornish conditions.

Seasonal & year-round planting

Garden Maintenance
